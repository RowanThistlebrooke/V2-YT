Drift Detection

Have your patterns quietly slipped?
A longer-horizon companion to Burnout Risk: instead of "is this week worse than last week" (acute), this asks whether your OWN recent baseline — the last days — has drifted from your OWN established pattern over the weeks before that. Each chart below shows the daily values with the recent window shaded and a dashed line marking your baseline average; a metric is flagged as real drift only once it's down at least 25% from that baseline, so one noisy stretch doesn't trigger a false alarm.
